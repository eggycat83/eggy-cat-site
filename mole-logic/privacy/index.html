Mole Logic Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Mole Logic is a logic puzzle game for teens and adults, made by Eggy Cat ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and how. In short: we don't collect your personal information. Your game data stays on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to show those ads, as described below.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, age, or other personal details.
  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own, and no servers that receive your game data.
  • No chat or contact with other players.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.

Information kept on your device

The app saves the following on your device only. It is never sent to us:

  • Your best times, daily dig results, and daily streak.
  • Games in progress, so you can continue them later.
  • Settings, such as whether sounds and vibration are on.
  • Whether you've bought "Remove ads".
  • Whether you've seen the How to play guide, and when the app last asked for a review.

Uninstalling the app removes this information. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include it in your device backup, which is handled by Google.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Mole Logic is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob: a short video you can choose to watch for an extra hint, and an occasional full-screen ad between games. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers.
  • Your IP address, which gives an approximate location.
  • Device and app information, such as the device model, operating system, and language.
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps.
  • Diagnostic information, such as ad loading errors.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. To learn more, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your ad choices

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form before showing ads. You can change your choice any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (on most Android phones: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Ads will then be less personalised.
  • Remove ads: a one-time purchase turns off ads in the app.

Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play.

  • We never see or receive card numbers or other payment details.
  • Google Play shares limited order information with us, such as an order number, the product, the price, and the country, so we can handle refunds and support. We do not use it for any other purpose.
  • Google's handling of purchase information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing your daily result

After the daily dig, you can share a short text with your result (the date, your time, hints used, and your streak). It only leaves your device if you choose an app to share it with, and then that app's privacy terms apply.

Ratings and reviews

The app may sometimes show Google Play's own review box after a win. Reviews are handled by Google Play. We don't see whether you rated the app or what you wrote, except as a public review on Google Play.

Internet use

The game works offline. The app makes no network requests of its own. Google AdMob and Google Play connect to the internet to show ads, ask for consent, and process and check purchases.

Age

Mole Logic is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to reply to you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
