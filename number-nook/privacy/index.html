Number Nook Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Number Nook is a number puzzle learning game for children ages 6–8, made by Eggy Cat ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and how. In short: Number Nook does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information, from children or from anyone else.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in. We never ask for a name, email address, age, or any other personal details.
  • No advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics, tracking, or third-party data collection tools.
  • No chat, sharing, or ways for children to contact others.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.

Information kept on the device

The app saves a small amount of information on the device only. It is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Whether sounds and vibration are turned on or off.
  • Whether the full version has been unlocked.

Uninstalling the app removes this information. Game progress and stars are not saved between sessions.

Read-aloud

When a question is read aloud, the app uses the text-to-speech engine built into the device. The text of the question is not sent to us. The speech engine is provided by the device or operating system maker and is covered by their privacy terms.

Purchases

Number Nook is free to download. A one-time purchase unlocks extra puzzle types. Purchases are made by a parent, behind a parent gate (a question a young child can't answer), and are processed entirely by Google Play.

  • We never see or receive card numbers or other payment details.
  • Google Play shares limited order information with us, such as an order number, the product, the price, and the country, so we can handle refunds and support. We do not use it for any other purpose.
  • Google's handling of purchase information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Internet use

The game works offline. The app does not make network requests of its own. Google Play may connect to the internet to process purchases and to check whether the full version was bought.

Children's privacy

Number Nook is designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app gives children no way to submit it. Purchases and settings are behind a parent gate. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to reply to you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your child's privacy: eggysupport@gmail.com
