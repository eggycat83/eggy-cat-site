Number Nook Support

Help for parents and teachers

Contact us

Email eggysupport@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and what happened. Please don't include your child's personal details.

The grown-ups area

Tap the grown-ups button (two people) in the top-left corner of the home screen and answer the question. There you can unlock the full version, restore a purchase, and turn sounds and vibration on or off.

Restoring a purchase

If you reinstalled the app or moved to a new phone, open the grown-ups area and tap Restore purchase. Make sure the phone is signed in to the same Google account that made the purchase. You won't be charged again.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Google Play. You can request a refund through Google Play; see Google Play refund help. If you have trouble, email us.

Read-aloud doesn't speak

Read-aloud uses the phone's own text-to-speech engine. Check that the device volume is up and that a text-to-speech voice is installed (on Android: Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech).
