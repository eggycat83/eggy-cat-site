How to play

Fill the grid so every row, column, and box holds each number once. Start in the Meadow on small 6×6 grids, or in the Woods if you already know Sudoku. Each land has nine puzzles, and every puzzle you solve uncovers a piece of the land's picture. Solve five to open the next land. Every puzzle can be solved by logic, with no guessing needed.

Stuck on a puzzle?

Tap Hint. It first tells you where to look, then Show why explains the reasoning, and Fill it in places the number. Hints are free and unlimited.

Removing ads and restoring a purchase

Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap Remove ads. If you reinstalled the app or moved to a new phone, tap Restore purchase instead. Make sure the phone is signed in to the same Google account that made the purchase. You won't be charged again.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Google Play. You can request a refund through Google Play; see Google Play refund help. If you have trouble, email us.

Changing your ad privacy choices

If you were asked for consent to ads, you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices. See the privacy policy for more.

An ad didn't load

Ads need an internet connection. If you chose to watch an ad to open a land and it can't load, the land opens anyway. The game itself works fully offline.
