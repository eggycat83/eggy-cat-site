Help with the game, ads, and purchases

Contact us

Email eggysupport@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and what happened.

How to play

Tap the ? button in the top-left corner of the home screen for a short guide. Each number tells you how many rocks are buried in the tiles touching it. Mark the rocks and dig up every other tile to clear the garden. Every garden can be solved by logic, with no guessing needed.

Removing ads and restoring a purchase

Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap Remove ads. If you reinstalled the app or moved to a new phone, tap Restore purchase instead. Make sure the phone is signed in to the same Google account that made the purchase. You won't be charged again.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Google Play. You can request a refund through Google Play; see Google Play refund help. If you have trouble, email us.

Changing your ad privacy choices

If you were asked for consent to ads, you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices. See the privacy policy for more.

A hint ad didn't load

Ads need an internet connection. If an ad can't load, you still get the hint. The game itself works fully offline.
